# language

- Ukrainian carries the body, rhythm, and primary meaning;
- English carries engineering terms, contracts, and precision;
- French may carry lightness and half-tone;
- other languages appear only where the text requires a deliberate register.

Rhythm is part of the interface. Precision is part of the rhythm.

## Stylistic locales

Languages define the broad linguistic roles of the writing system. Stylistic locales define narrower, explicitly selected authorial registers within those languages.

Locale contracts live under [`locales/`](locales/README.md). They may shape pacing, syntax, address, punctuation, and lexical judgment, but they do not override grammatical correctness, factual precision, or exact engineering terminology.
