# publishing

0x0sky publishes through a distributed set of surfaces rather than a single canonical feed.

Publishing may span:

- technical and engineering writing;
- research notes and speculative thinking;
- professional writing;
- poetry and literary translation;
- visual and audiovisual work;
- music and cultural curation;
- personal and experimental publishing;
- organizational communication.

A publishing surface may have its own identity, audience, language, format, cadence, and editorial rules.

The same underlying idea may be projected differently across surfaces. These projections are not required to be literal copies or translations.

`mind` describes the durable identity, language systems, and principles behind publishing. Individual platforms, channels, accounts, posts, audience metrics, and transient publishing strategy remain external.

Publishing surfaces are projections of the subject, not canonical identity records of their own. A surface may appear, disappear, split by locale, change platform, or become dormant without changing the durable meaning declared by this repository.
